VOICES - Linux (x86_64)
=======================

VST3 PLUGINS
  mkdir -p ~/.vst3
  cp -r Voices.vst3 "Voices FX.vst3" ~/.vst3/
  Rescan plugins in your DAW (Reaper, Bitwig, Ardour...).
    Voices     = instrument (choir / vocal synth / vocoder from MIDI, side-chain voice input)
    Voices FX  = insert effect for vocal tracks (auto-tune, voice changer; also takes MIDI)

STANDALONE
  ./Voices
  Options -> Audio/MIDI Settings: choose ALSA or JACK, your microphone input and
  your MIDI keyboard. Use headphones, then untick "Mute audio input" (Settings... on
  the yellow bar) to hear yourself live.

  Needs: ALSA (libasound2), FreeType, X11 - present on any desktop distro.
  JACK is used if installed.
