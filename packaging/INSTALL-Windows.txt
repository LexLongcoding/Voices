VOICES - Windows (64-bit)
=========================

WHAT'S IN HERE
  Voices.vst3      the instrument: choir, vocal synth and vocoder played from MIDI,
                   with a side-chain input for your voice (vocoder / auto-tune).
  Voices FX.vst3   the same engine as an insert effect: put it on a vocal track for
                   auto-tune and the voice changer (it also accepts MIDI).
  Voices.exe       standalone app: sing into your microphone, play the on-screen keys
                   or a MIDI keyboard, hear everything live.

VST3 PLUGINS
  1. Copy BOTH "Voices.vst3" and "Voices FX.vst3" folders to:
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
     (Windows will ask for administrator permission - click Continue.)
  2. In your DAW, rescan plugins. "Voices" appears as an instrument,
     "Voices FX" as an effect.

STANDALONE (hear yourself live)
  Double-click Voices.exe - no install needed.
  1. Click "Options" (top-left) -> "Audio/MIDI Settings...":
       - pick your audio interface (ASIO or "Windows Audio (Exclusive Mode)" for low latency)
       - pick your microphone input
       - tick your MIDI keyboard under "Active MIDI inputs" (optional)
  2. JUCE mutes the input at first to avoid feedback ("Audio input is muted...").
     Use HEADPHONES, then click "Settings..." on that yellow bar and untick "Mute audio input".

  Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the app
  isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click "More info" ->
  "Run anyway". This only happens once.

PATCHES
  Your saved patches live in  %APPDATA%\Voices Synth\Patches
  The Standalone and both plugins share the same patches.
